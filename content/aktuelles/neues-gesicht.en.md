As of today, the new website of The Future is online. It looks different, but above all it says more clearly what we stand for: artificial intelligence and robotics can create prosperity for everyone. For that to happen, Switzerland needs a plan. The website should be able to show that plan in three seconds and explain it in three hours.

---

## First the stance, then the depth

The old website was a sequence of slides. You scrolled, a page snapped into place, the next one followed. It felt calm, but it hid two things: our message and the depth behind it.

The new homepage works in layers. At the very top is what we want: "The Future. We build it." and three phrases that sum up our stance: prosperity over scarcity thinking, security over fear, sovereignty over dependence. Below come the ten theses of the [manifesto](@/manifest.md), then all thirteen pillars of the [programme](@/programm.md) at a glance, then the evidence from the [Future Lab](@/zukunftslabor/_index.md). If you only have a minute, you will understand us. If you have an evening, you will find the sources.

![The new homepage: wordmark, core statement and the Swiss cross made of cubes in the background](/aktuelles/neues-gesicht/startseite.webp)

## A tangible future instead of science fiction

The website was meant to feel futuristic, but not kitschy. No neon colours, no robot eyes. We took our cues from things you can touch: a light ground like ceramic, dark ink, fine lines as on a construction drawing. Behind every page stands the same object, a three-dimensional Swiss cross made of cubes. It turns slowly, responds to scrolling and steps back as soon as the text takes over.

The new colour is called **Alpenglühen** (alpenglow): pink, magenta, violet and indigo, like the mountains in the first light of day. It stands for optimism without pathos and is clearly distinct from the colours of the established parties.

The new icon, the **Würfelkreuz** (cube cross), is made of seven cubes. A Swiss cross assembled from building blocks: that is exactly what we intend to do in politics.

---

## How we decided

We did not decide at the drawing board, but by direct comparison. For the wordmark we put fifteen variants side by side, for the colour nine palettes, for the icon fourteen drafts, each one large and small, light and dark, in all four languages. Some things we tried and dropped again, such as a red full stop in the wordmark. What remained is what works just as well on a phone as on a poster.

![Nine colour palettes compared, here the chosen Alpenglühen](/aktuelles/neues-gesicht/paletten.webp)

It is the same method we propose for politics: lay the options side by side, test them, decide, and document openly why.

## What is new

- **The vision as a blueprint.** The [vision](@/vision.md) shows "the abundance stack": from energy through compute, sovereign AI and talent to application in every sector and a dividend for everyone. With figures, sources and targets with dates attached.
- **A new pillar.** [Sovereign AI & Compute](@/souveraene-ki.md) now comes first in the programme, because everything else builds on it.
- **The manifesto, version 2.0.** Ten theses, each with a permanent address you can link to and quote.
- **Pinning.** You can pin any section and find it again later. The pins stay in your browser, with no account and no cookies, and can be shared as a link.
- **Calmer on your phone.** Many of you complained about the "magnetic" snapping while scrolling, especially on the iPhone. It is gone. The page scrolls freely, and forms no longer zoom in uninvited.

![The new website on the iPhone](/aktuelles/neues-gesicht/iphone.webp)

## Built in the open

Every section has an address in the corner, for example 0x1002 for the first thesis. That gives away how we work: like an open software project. The website's source code, the design and all brand assets are public on [GitHub](https://github.com/zukunft-ch). At the bottom of every page you can see how much it weighs, measured in your browser, and that it runs without trackers.

If you find a mistake, have a better wording or want to improve a translation, you can contribute directly, with an issue or a pull request. A party that calls for open government should work openly itself.

The future is a construction project. So is this website. [Build it with us.](@/mitmachen.md)
