## Privacy policy

### Responsible party

The Future (Die Zukunft)
Basel, Switzerland
Email: [hey@die-zukunft.ch](mailto:hey@die-zukunft.ch)

### Principle

The protection of your personal data is important to us. We only collect data that is necessary for the operation of this website and our political work.

### Contact form

When you contact us via the contact form, your details (name, email, message) are processed by our own application running on Cloudflare infrastructure. This data is used exclusively to handle your enquiry and is not shared with third parties.

### Newsletter

When you sign up for our newsletter, we store your email address. You can unsubscribe at any time. We use your address exclusively for sending our updates.

### Hosting and fonts

This website is statically hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure. No cookies are set and no tracking tools are used. All fonts, scripts and resources are served from our own domain — we do not embed any external CDNs or third-party services that could receive your IP address.

### Pins and settings on your device

If you pin sections or switch the colour theme, your browser stores this locally (localStorage, keys `dz-pins-v1` and `dz-theme`). This information stays on your device, is never transmitted to us or to third parties, and is not a cookie. You delete it by removing the pins or clearing the site data in your browser. If you share a pin link (`#pins=…`), it contains only the addresses of the pinned sections.

### Your rights

You have the right at any time to request information about your stored data, its correction or deletion. Please contact us at the address above.

### Changes

We reserve the right to adapt this privacy policy as needed. The current version can always be found on this page.
