Switzerland votes on federal proposals four times a year. This page is a permanent overview: for each voting Sunday, a short framing per proposal along the twelve pillars of our [program](@/programm.en.md). We deliberately don't issue voting recommendations yet: ballot positions belong to a democratic party process with members and assemblies — and as a young party, we are building exactly that. Until then, we describe where a proposal meets the program and what tension sits behind it.

---

## Next vote — 27 September 2026

Two popular initiatives are on the ballot. The full framing is in the article [27 September 2026 vote: two initiatives, three pillars](@/aktuelles/abstimmung-27-september-2026.en.md).

**Popular initiative "Preserving Swiss neutrality (Neutrality Initiative)".** Seeks to enshrine perpetual, armed neutrality in the constitution and to rule out military alliances and coercive measures not mandated by the UN. The goals largely overlap with our program — the tension lies in the sanctions clause and in the ban on cooperation, which could hamper defensive cooperation against hybrid threats. → Pillars [Neutrality & peace](@/neutralitaet-frieden.en.md) and [Energy & resilience](@/energy-resilience.en.md). Federal Council and Parliament: no.

**Popular initiative "For secure nutrition (Nutrition Initiative)".** Seeks a net self-sufficiency rate of at least 70 percent within ten years, more plant-based food and clean drinking water. Names a real dependence but sets a target without a plan: supply becomes more secure through technology and stockpiling, not through a number in the constitution. → Pillars [Energy & resilience](@/energy-resilience.en.md) and [Life science & health](@/life-science.en.md). Federal Council and Parliament: no.

## Planned — 29 November 2026

Four proposals are on the ballot. The full framing is in the article [29 November 2026 vote: four proposals, five pillars](@/aktuelles/abstimmung-29-november-2026.en.md).

**Federal decree on additional financing of the AHV through a VAT increase.** Seeks to top up old-age pension funding through a higher VAT; as a constitutional amendment it goes to a mandatory popular vote. Consumption rather than wages as the financing base fits our logic — the tension is the partial funding: about a third of the cost, the rest stays open. → Pillars [Basic income](@/grundeinkommen.en.md) and [Open government](@/offener-staat.en.md). Federal Council and Parliament: yes.

**Popular initiative "For a restriction of fireworks".** Seeks a nationwide ban on the sale and use of loud fireworks by private individuals. Names a real problem of densified cities — the tension is the level: a constitutional article for a rule municipalities can set themselves today. → Pillar [Cities & housing](@/stadte-wohnen.en.md). Federal Council and Parliament: no.

**Popular initiative "Yes to fair federal taxes for married couples too – end the discrimination of marriage!".** Seeks to abolish the marriage penalty in direct federal tax while keeping joint assessment of married couples — a different model from the individual taxation accepted in March. The goal is settled — the tension is the duplication: two systems for the same people, with losses of up to 1.4 billion. → Pillar [Open government](@/offener-staat.en.md). Federal Council and Parliament: no.

**Amendment to the Federal Act on War Material.** Seeks to allow the Federal Council to deviate from the licensing criteria for war material exports in special cases; a referendum brings the proposal to the people. Protective capability needs an industrial base — the tension is control: executive discretion instead of clear rules in the law. → Pillars [Neutrality & peace](@/neutralitaet-frieden.en.md) and [Energy & resilience](@/energy-resilience.en.md). Federal Council and Parliament: yes.

## Last vote — 14 June 2026

Two proposals, two results. The full framing is in the article [14 June 2026 vote: two proposals, three pillars](@/aktuelles/abstimmung-14-juni-2026.en.md).

**Popular initiative "No to a Switzerland with 10 million!"** A rigid population cap pulls the wrong lever: the honest problem is infrastructure, not headcount. → Pillars [Talent & integration](@/talent-integration.en.md) and [Cities & housing](@/stadte-wohnen.en.md). People: no (54.8%).

**Amendment to the Civilian Service Act.** Higher admission hurdles to civilian service — real tension between army strength and civil resilience. → Pillar [Neutrality & peace](@/neutralitaet-frieden.en.md). People: yes (53.0%).

## Before that — 8 March 2026

Five proposals, three results that count long-term.

**Cash initiative.** Anchor cash in the constitution. → Pillar [Digital sovereignty](@/digitale-souveraenitaet.en.md). People: no.

**Cash counter-proposal.** Protect cash without a rigid quantity rule. → Pillar [Digital sovereignty](@/digitale-souveraenitaet.en.md). People: yes.

**SBC initiative.** Cap the radio and TV licence fee at 200 francs per year. → Pillar [Language & understanding](@/sprache-verstaendigung.en.md). People: no.

**Climate Fund initiative.** Federal fund for climate-protection investments. → Pillar [Energy & resilience](@/energy-resilience.en.md). People: no.

**Individual taxation.** Separate tax assessment for married couples. → Pillar [Open state](@/offener-staat.en.md). People: yes.

## Why this tracker exists

Federal popular votes are Switzerland's most important steering instrument. They come in dense succession — four dates a year, often two to five proposals per date. Anyone who does not follow politics full-time loses the overview fast.

This page is our contribution to civil attention: a compact framing per proposal, always along the same twelve pillars. No voting recommendation. For the full text and official arguments, see [admin.ch](https://www.admin.ch/) and [easyvote.ch](https://www.easyvote.ch/).
